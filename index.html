About

Bridging the science of how we learn with the demands of clinical practice

With over two decades of cardiovascular nursing experience and a sustained commitment to nursing education, I work at a place few inhabit — where clinical expertise, cognitive science, and pedagogy converge. I hold a Master of Science in Nursing and am currently completing doctoral study in education, with a research focus on how nurses learn to think under pressure.

My classroom work spans pre-licensure nursing programs and continuing education for practicing clinicians. I bring the same precision to curriculum design that I bring to a cardiac monitor: what matters is pattern recognition, and patterns must be taught deliberately.

cognitivenursing.io is my professional home — a space for writing, collaboration, and inquiry about the cognitive dimensions of nursing education and clinical reasoning.
