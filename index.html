About

Bridging the science of how we learn with the demands of clinical practice.

With more than two decades of cardiovascular and critical care nursing behind me, I work where clinical expertise, cognitive science, and teaching meet.

I hold a Master of Science in Nursing and the PCCN certification, still work PRN shifts in the ICU, and am beginning doctoral study in Educational Psychology at Regent University. My classroom work spans pre-licensure nursing programs and continuing education for practicing clinicians.

cognitivenursing.io is my professional home: a place for writing, collaboration, and inquiry into the cognitive side of nursing education and clinical reasoning.

I bring the same precision to curriculum that I bring to a cardiac monitor: what matters is pattern recognition, and patterns must be taught deliberately.
