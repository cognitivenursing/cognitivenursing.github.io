4 East Multi-Patient Delegation Case

A standardized multi-patient case for assessing scope of practice, assignment, and delegation decisions.

One six-patient progressive care census, built twice: once with the student as the RN team leader (ADN) and once as the LPN on an RN-led team (PN). Both versions are scored on the same five-criterion rubric, so results can be compared across programs. Each case runs in a simulated EHR, unfolds in three timed phases, and ends with a copy-and-paste submission for the course dropbox.

For faculty

Answers save only in the student's browser, and the Phase 1 lock can be reset by clearing site data. For scores you report as program outcome data, have students complete the case in a proctored setting. The faculty scoring key is open during faculty review and will be protected before students use the case.

Patients, staff, the hospital, and unit policies are fictional. Scope references are North Carolina-specific (NC Nursing Practice Act and NCBON NA I / NA II task lists).
