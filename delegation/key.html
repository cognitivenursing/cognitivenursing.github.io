Phase 1: patient-level decisions

First patient to see: 403 Tillery (new admission, ACS not ruled out, no nursing assessment, troponin pending). Also defensible: 401 Benfield, if the student says the K⁺ 3.2 and Mg 1.6 must reach the provider before the 0900 furosemide and digoxin, and plans to see Tillery next. Any other first choice scores 2 or lower on criterion 1.

Score 3 on criterion 3 when every patient decision is correct and names at least the right task, circumstance, and person. Score 4 when the student applies all five rights to every decision. Score 5 when the student also separates assignment (RN → LPN, within LPN scope) from delegation (licensed nurse → NA), and addresses 4 East policy and Brenda's currency.

SBAR (criterion 5).

  • ADN: all four parts. S: acute dyspnea, hypoxemia, tachycardia. B: POD 2 THA, SCD refusal, calf ache. A: suspected PE. R: bedside evaluation now, plus a specific request such as CTA or orders.
  • PN: all four parts. A states the data and that it is urgent, without a medical diagnosis. R asks Dana to come now.
  • A missing part scores 2.

Reflection. Credit any filter that is named correctly and supported by the case. The strongest reflections pick 401 (policy is stricter than law), 406 (competence: Brenda's gap), or 403 for PN (law: a co-signature does not create scope).
