Add it to Gmail

  1. Open this page in Chrome on your computer.
  2. Click and drag from the left edge of the logo to the end of the last line, so the whole signature is highlighted, then copy (Ctrl+C or Cmd+C).
  3. In Gmail, open Settings, then See all settings, and scroll to Signature.
  4. Create a new signature, click in the box, and paste (Ctrl+V or Cmd+V).
  5. Under Signature defaults, choose it for new emails and replies, then click Save Changes at the bottom.

Send yourself a test and check it on your phone too. If the logo pastes in large, click it in the signature box and choose Small.
