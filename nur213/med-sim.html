    About this tool: limits, sources, AI disclosure

    Simulation, not physiology software. Rhythms and vital-sign responses are scripted teaching approximations. Real responses vary in timing and size, and drug effects are compressed in time (the log notes when time is skipped). The ECG is an idealized lead II. Use the tool to practice reasoning, not to predict how a real patient will respond.

    Scope. Every medication action assumes a provider order or an approved protocol. The RN decisions here are assessing, recognizing, holding and clarifying, administering safely, and escalating. Doses follow AHA 2025 ACLS where it specifies them. Always follow the facility's protocols.

    Sources checked (October 2026). AHA 2025 Guidelines for CPR & ECC, adult ALS, including the tachyarrhythmia algorithm and the bradycardia and cardiac arrest recommendations (magnesium not routine in arrest; amiodarone or lidocaine for refractory VF/pVT; no verapamil/diltiazem for wide-complex tachycardia; avoid CCBs and IV beta-blockers in decompensated HF with LV systolic dysfunction; avoid AV-nodal blockers and IV amiodarone in pre-excited AF). 2023 ACC/AHA/ACCP/HRS AF guideline (DOACs preferred over warfarin except with mechanical valves or mitral stenosis; aspirin is not a substitute for anticoagulation). Modified Valsalva conversion rate from the REVERT trial (Appelboam et al., Lancet 2015).
