    About this tool: limits, sources, AI disclosure

    Simulated strips. Every tracing is generated mathematically as an idealized lead II at 25 mm/s and 10 mm/mV. It is not a recording from a patient. Real strips have artifact, baseline wander, lead-to-lead variation, and messier morphology. Use this tool alongside real rhythm strips, not in place of them. The pulse (pleth) trace is a teaching schematic. It is not a quantitative waveform.

    Scope. Interventions are written at the RN level ("prepare," "anticipate," "per order/protocol"). Medication decisions belong to the provider and facility protocol. This tool does not replace BLS/ACLS training.

    Sources checked. AHA 2025 Guidelines for CPR & ECC, adult advanced life support and bradycardia (atropine 1 mg IV every 3–5 min, max 3 mg): cpr.heart.org algorithms. 2023 ACC/AHA/ACCP/HRS Atrial Fibrillation Guideline (aspirin, alone or with clopidogrel, is not recommended in place of anticoagulation for stroke prevention): ACC key points.
